# Anti-AI Slop

Use this when a design feels generic, glossy, over-explained, blob-heavy, or like an AI SaaS template.

## Immediate Rejects

- Centered headline, pill, CTA pair, and gradient blob as the whole hero.
- Purple/blue mesh gradient as the main design idea.
- Three cards with icons and short blurbs as the main section.
- Fake dashboard with vague labels.
- Generic split hero: left copy, right framed preview, loose stats, soft grid/gradient.
- Framed app preview used as wallpaper instead of a real artifact.
- Placeholder brand: single initial mark plus generic product category.
- Metrics row with numbers that do not connect to visible product state.
- Background treatment acting as the whole art direction.
- Huge rounded containers nested inside huge rounded containers.
- Dark glass panels with no product specificity.
- Decorative SVG waves/orbs/blobs replacing real media or product UI.
- Motion added before hierarchy, copy, or product evidence is strong.

## Replace With

- A real product artifact.
- A selected object, task, output, transcript, table/detail state, or before/after.
- A sharper content promise.
- A domain-specific layout lane.
- A clear before/after workflow.
- Real media or generated bitmap imagery.
- A denser, more useful product surface.
- One distinctive material or typographic choice.

## Slop Questions

Ask before finishing:

- Could this page sell any SaaS if I changed the logo?
- Is the biggest thing on the page also the most useful thing?
- Does the user need to read a paragraph before seeing proof?
- Are there more decorative elements than product elements?
- Do sections repeat the same grid/card shape?
- Is mobile just a long pile of desktop cards?

If yes, redesign the structure before adding polish.

## Interaction Trap

Interaction can make a good design feel alive. It cannot make a weak design good.

Do not add hover effects to avoid solving:

- Bad copy.
- Weak product artifact.
- Broken mobile layout.
- Poor hierarchy.
- Generic art direction.
