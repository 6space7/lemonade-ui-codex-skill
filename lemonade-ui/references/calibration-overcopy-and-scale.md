# Calibration: Overcopy And Scale

Use this when the design is mostly giant text, explanatory paragraphs, stacked claims, or type that shouts without proving anything.

## Failure Pattern

- Headline takes most of the viewport but says a generic promise.
- Paragraph explains the workflow that the UI should demonstrate.
- CTAs, stats, badges, and nav all compete with the same intensity.
- Mobile becomes a tall wall of copy before the useful artifact appears.

## Better Direction

- Make the product artifact the visual anchor.
- Shorten the headline until it names one concrete promise.
- Convert paragraph content into labels, rows, states, counts, or controls inside the UI.
- Use large type only when the surrounding product proof can still breathe.

## Implementation Cues

- Hero headline: 4-8 words when possible.
- Hero support copy: one sentence under 18 words when possible.
- On mobile, reduce words before shrinking everything.
- Give compact UI panels smaller headings than page-level sections.
- Put proof near the first CTA: count, status, preview, quote, file, price, or outcome.

## Checklist

- Does the first viewport show what the product is, not just what it claims?
- Would the page still work if the paragraph disappeared?
- Is the largest text earning its size?
- Can mobile reach product proof without scrolling through a speech?
