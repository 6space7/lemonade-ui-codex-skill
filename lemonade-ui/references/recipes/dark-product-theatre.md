# Recipe: Dark Product Theatre

Use when the page should feel cinematic, premium, technical, focused, or distinctly Lemonade.

## Structure

- Use a black or near-black stage as the main visual field.
- Let one product artifact dominate the first viewport.
- Keep the headline short and poster-like.
- Place proof inside or around the artifact: status chips, selected states, notes, cursors, labels, timestamps, outputs.
- Use glow, grain, light beams, reflections, or edge atmosphere sparingly to light the artifact.
- Let the next section peek below the hero so the page feels deep, not poster-only.

## Layout Moves

- Centered headline above a large cropped artifact.
- Offset headline with a massive artifact crossing the centerline.
- Artifact first, with copy tucked into a side rail or top caption.
- One lit panel with two small floating proof fragments.
- Full-bleed dark stage with the product object emerging from below the fold.

## Type

- Headline: 2 to 4 short lines.
- Body: one quiet sentence.
- Labels: small, exact, and product-specific.
- Avoid long paragraphs on dark backgrounds.

## Material

- Near-black stage, subtle border lines, soft inner shadows.
- One vivid accent: green, red-orange, electric blue, mint, or hot yellow.
- Product surfaces can be dark glass, matte panels, terminal-like panes, or crisp white cards inside the dark stage.

## Mobile

- Show a cropped artifact or selected object in the first viewport.
- Reduce headline scale before pushing proof below the fold.
- Hide secondary floating fragments if they make the artifact cramped.
- Keep the primary action near the product moment.

## Avoid

- A dark background with generic cards floating randomly.
- Glow as the whole idea.
- Tiny whole-product previews.
- Huge headline followed by the artifact only after scrolling.
- Low-contrast gray text that becomes unreadable on mobile.
