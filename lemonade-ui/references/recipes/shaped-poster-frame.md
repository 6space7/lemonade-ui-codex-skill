# Recipe: Shaped Poster Frame

Use when the page needs a strong silhouette: carved containers, capsule nav, giant rounded fields, cutouts, or a product poster feel.

## Structure

- Create one oversized frame or field that defines the page.
- Put the product object inside, across, or breaking out of the frame.
- Let the frame shape be meaningful, not just rounded decoration.
- Use the frame to organize navigation, hero copy, product proof, and next-section hint.

## Layout Moves

- Giant rounded field with carved top or bottom cutouts.
- Capsule nav embedded into the frame edge.
- Product card/device floating over a bold color field.
- Dark inner stage inside a light outer shell.
- Poster frame with one clipped product crop and bottom proof strip.

## Type

- Use confident type, but do not let it swallow the frame.
- Break the headline deliberately.
- Keep body copy short enough for the frame and product object to remain the hero.

## Material

- Bold brand field, matte black insert, glass object, paper shell, chrome controls, or soft plastic buttons.
- Use one accent system across frame edges, buttons, and proof chips.

## Mobile

- Preserve the silhouette in a simpler form.
- Use a single shaped container, not many nested rounded blocks.
- Place the product object before secondary copy.
- Keep carved/cutout details large enough to read.

## Avoid

- Every card using the same large radius.
- Rounded rectangles inside rounded rectangles with no hierarchy.
- Shape that causes awkward clipping or text overflow.
- Decorative frame with no product evidence inside it.
